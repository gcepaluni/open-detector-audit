Public schools across the region adopted new software quickly during the last decade. Teachers welcomed tools that saved time, but few had the chance to test them on their own students before using them in class.

The question for any school is simple. When a tool makes a judgement about a student, how often is it wrong, and who bears the cost of the mistake? A wrong grade can be fixed. A wrong accusation follows a student for years.
